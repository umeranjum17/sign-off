# Summary home, area screens and Triage

Covers the top of the board in `web/index.html`: the Summary (four area tiles, Needs you first, Today, a short list per area), Triage inside Waiting on you (one ask at a time), report cards on Reports and explainers, the area screens in the URL hash (`#waiting`, `#evidence`, `#reports`, `#answered`) with `‹ Summary` on a phone and the sidebar at 60rem and wider, the full trade-off, evidence captures framed at their own shape, and recommended versus picked buttons. Follow Launch and Drive in `SKILL.md` first.

Each item lives in one area: an answered item in **Answered**; an evidence update in **Evidence**; an explainer, an item with nothing to answer, or any item on a read-only board in **Reports and explainers**; every other open ask in **Waiting on you**, oldest first. An open item with `"area"` set (see `docs/file-formats.md`) shows in that area instead. An item with `"steps"` draws them on its report card.

Build an answers-only board (`post --from <file> --replace`) oldest first: an `interactive` page with six `steps` (marks `store`, `ai`, `gate`, `store`+`fail`, none, `fail`), an `interactive` page without steps, an `interactive` page with `"area": "evidence"`, and a `diagram` with `"options": []`, two yes/no decisions, a three-option design with image mocks, three evidence updates (one with two portrait shots, one landscape), a two-option design with image mocks and `--recommend a`, a diagram ask with options `yes=Yes, build it` and `needs-work=Needs work`, and a yes/no decision. Answer the first two decisions and the three-option design through `POST /api/answers`. Widths: phone `390x844x3,mobile,touch`, desktop `1440x900x1`.
