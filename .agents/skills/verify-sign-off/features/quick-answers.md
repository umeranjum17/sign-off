# Quick answers: buttons first, Other answer last, one tap with Undo, recommend required

Covers the rule that typing is the last resort: every answerable card shows its option buttons first, the recommended option first and marked **Recommended**; every item that asks something, a design's Picks included, has a text box (none when an option is valued `text`) folded behind **Other answer** (**Write a reply** when it is a reply item with no options) and never open by default; one tap saves at once and a toast offers **Undo** for 5 seconds; `raise`/`post` refuse listed options with no recommendation. Follow Launch and Drive in `SKILL.md` first. Work in a fresh scratch folder and delete it at the end.

Write `$SCR/q/feed.json`: `{"schema": 2, "answers_only": true, "items": [<"type": "ask", "title": "Say yes or no: ...", "reply": true>, <"type": "ask", "reply": true, "options": [today=Today, friday=Friday, next-week="Next week"], "recommend_value": "friday">, <"type": "ask", "reply": true, "options": [done=Done, not-now="Not now"]>]}`, run `node dist/cli.js post $SCR/q/board.json --from $SCR/q/feed.json --replace` and serve it. `FIT` is the one in [dashboard.md](dashboard.md).

| 7 | Design card on a normal board (a `post --type design` with three options and `--recommend`, plus one `raise --option ... --recommend` ask), phone and desktop, light and dark | Both cards show **Other answer** after their buttons (after the Picks on the design card), folded, and it is the card's only place to type: no separate note link; open, it holds one box with **Send reply**, the **Another round** chip and **Send as a comment**.
