# Decide

The Decide tab (`#waiting`) and an ask (`#decide/<key>`), phone (390) first, built on the new look's shared parts (`web/ui.css`) to match the approved prototype (`signoff-restyle-board/proto/index.html`, frames `v2/review/phone/{decide,decide-priority,ask,ask-picked}.<theme>.png`) in light and dark. On a phone an ask is a sheet over the list; on a desk the list sits beside the ask (the desk keeps its earlier layout in the new parts until the desk pass).

## Sub-features

- Head: the title Decide in the shared title row; on a phone the round order button (the lines drawing, ink while By priority is on) opening a sheet, Order, of Newest and By priority (the one on, checked), then the round 40px light-or-dark button at its right edge (the same one as Today's: a moon in light, a sun in dark, kept in this browser).
- On a phone, Asks and Designs under the head, a segmented control each with its count (the asks waiting, the designs in review or draft): Designs opens [Designs](designs.md) as Decide's second list, Decide lit in the dock. Design reviews stay out of the red count.
- Newest / By priority: the order sheet on a phone, a segmented control under the head on a desk. Newest is the board's order (a sign-in first); By priority puts P0 first, then P1 to P3, newest first within one, a card with no priority last. The choice is kept in this browser (`localStorage` `sign-off-decide-sort`). Later always sits at the foot.
- Hero: "Waiting on you" over the count of open asks not in Later, a raised chip "N answered today" when any were, and "N for later" at the right when any are; under the answered chip the leash chip, "Leash: <When it matters>" (on a desk after "N asks wait on you · N answered today"), which opens the Leash in a sheet (Ask first, When it matters, Act, then tell) that saves `leash:` in config.yaml, an answers-only board too.
- The leash acting (`src/cli.ts` leashTake, the board's timer and raise): on When it matters the open board takes an ask's default at its silent_by (also one that fell due while it was closed); on Act, then tell me a raise takes the agent's recommended pick at once; Ask me first takes nothing. A guarded ask (`--guard`, or words of money, deleting, what can't be undone, keys or access) and every kind but a plain ask always wait. **Taken without you**, under the asks with its count, lists each answer the leash took until its card is opened (a `seen` mark on the trail, so on any device): its project tile, the question, "Taken by default at <time>" or "Agent picked <answer>", and on a phone a Change pill. Its card: "Answer", "Taken by default at <time>" (a clock) or "Agent picked <answer>, tell me if wrong" (a filled sparkle), the reason, the trail from Taken or Picked to Delivered, Change. An open guarded ask says "Always waits for you. It <spends money>, so no default or agent pick is ever taken." in place of its quiet line.
- Asks: a section head, then one card of 68px rows (`uiRow`): the ask's 40px icon tile (its project's own icon where the board holds one, `GET /api/project-icon`; a quiet project outline when no icon is available), the question on one line with a P0-P3 chip only when the card has a priority, then a 4px bar with its percent once the card's ticket has any progress (a full bar at 100%), else whose it is and when it was filed (today's time, Yesterday, or the date; a sign-in counts its link down, and reads Expired past it). An ask filed by a tool that is also a project on the board (its icon set with `project`) takes that project's tile. Nothing waiting: the card says All clear and "Nothing is waiting on you, <name>."
- Later and Answered: their own section heads with a count and cards of the same rows; an answered row has a check tile, the question, then the answer with its note and when ("Changed to <answer>" after a change), opens its ask, and never swipes. The last card clears the dock.
- Swipe: right answers with the recommended pick, left moves the ask to Later (on a Later row it brings it back), both over an ink ground as Reports' swipes. The row follows the finger; past a third of its width the action grows (a haptic tick on Android) and commits on release; a fast fling commits sooner; short of it the row springs back. A row with nothing to answer resists a right swipe.
- More, under the list as Today's: one quiet line, "Answer all recommended and Move all to later", opening a sheet with both (each with how many it would move, off when it has nothing to do). Each change shows a toast with Undo.
- An ask on a phone: a sheet rises over the dimmed list (the dock under the scrim): a grab handle, a head of the ask's small tile, whose it is and when, More (Discard, which asks for a reason: [Discard](discard.md)) and Close; the question wrapping in full; "Waiting on you since <date time>"; why; its preview (mocks, a page, a capture); one inset panel of what it carries, only when it carries something: Priority with its chip, and Its ask (the card's ticket: its title, a bar and percent once it has progress, else Not started) that opens the ticket's page; the proof it rests on (a small capture, its title and result; a tap opens the capture in a sheet); Your answer as one inset group of rows, each the answer and what it does with a radio at the right, Recommended a white tag, Your own answer last (its box opens under the group); one note ("A note for the agent, optional"); what happens if you stay quiet; its Activity (posted, answers, a change, the agent picking it up, comments); and at the sheet's foot Ask me later (white) beside one ink button, faint "Pick an answer" until a pick and then "Answer <pick>". Close, a tap on the dimmed list, or Escape closes it and the focus returns to its row; the list stays where it was scrolled.
- Answering: the button fills with the bar's ink and draws a check, the sheet closes, the row tints and folds away, and the toast (a check, the question, the answer, Undo, a countdown hairline) rises. Ask me later closes the sheet and moves the row to Later, with Undo.
- Typing on the next ask keeps its focus, draft and text selection when the previous answer's follow-up comment response arrives. The existing pick-one browser journey holds a real HTTP response until typing has begun, then releases it and checks all three.
- An answered ask (from the Answered group or Today's Cleared today): Answered (or Changed) and when, the answer in an inset card with its note and whether the agent has read it, "Before: ..." after a change, its Activity, and Change answer, which brings the rows back with the answer given tagged Your answer; a change is sent as one that replaces the earlier answer, and its toast has Undo.
- A sign-in, in the same sheet: its key in a tile, "Sign in to <service>", why it waits, Unlocks / Expires / Where as one inset group, the panel, Open sign-in and **Ask for a new link** (hidden on a card that runs `--start`), and under them what opening does. Expires reads the poster's own clock (`19:35`) with the time left counting down under the hour (`19:35 · 04:12 left`), and **Expired** past it, with Open off and the value and copy button still there. Tapping Open marks the card **Opened — waiting for approval**, never done, until the poster runs `signin done`; Ask for a new link posts a comment the poster reads with `poll`/`wait` and then reads **Asked for a new link**. Light and dark at 390 in `signin-expiry-shots.mjs <folder> <light|dark> 390` (valid, near-expiry, opened, asked, expired); test/design.test.mjs (the sign-in card test) is the regression.
- A sign-in's long values: each fact row keeps its label at its own width on one line and gives the value the rest of the row, so an 85-character Unlocks value wraps inside it rather than squeezing "Unlocks" into a column of letters; a value that cannot wrap cleanly (a link, a long host) stays on one line with an ellipsis beside a 44px copy button (`aria-label` "Copy Unlocks"/"Copy Where") that hands over the whole value and says Copied for a moment. Nothing in the sheet passes its right edge at 360, 390 or 445 on a phone or at 1280 and 1440 on a desk. Long links, the owner's own Unlocks line and the copy button's clipboard text are measured by `signin-shots.mjs <folder> <light|dark> <360|390|445|1280|1440>`; test/design.test.mjs (the sign-in card test) is the regression.
- On a desk (1024px and wider) the list (the same head and control, no hero, and the asks' head with their count inside their card, Asks 4, as the prototype's list card; a title wraps to two lines and the row grows to hold it) sits beside the selected ask, drawn as the prototype's open ask ([Card detail](card-detail.md)): its head line (its kind, who asked and when, More), the ask beside its Activity, and the answers pinned at its foot as cards over the note, Ask me later (L, which steps on to the next ask) and Answer; J and K walk the list, a number picks, Enter answers and selects the next, Cmd+Z, C, the ? sheet.
- Reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`): no rises, sheet slides, pops or springs; every state change still happens.

## Driving it

1. `npm run build`.
2. Stills beside the prototype, measured: `TMPDIR=/tmp node .agents/skills/done-done-verify-sign-off/decide-newlook-shots.mjs <folder> <prototype index.html>` seeds its own board (the prototype's four asks: a P0 fix plan on a ticket 26% done, the Mac screen recording grant, a P1 yes or no, yesterday's yes or no; two asks answered today; two projects with icons), serves and stops it, and captures decide, decide-priority, ask, ask-picked, ask-ticket and ask-answered at 390 in light and dark, decide and ask-picked at 1280, and decide-ticket-done (the P0 ask's ticket moved to 100%: a full bar reading 100%), each with `.measure.json` (one left and right edge, the 4px grid, 68px rows with a 40px tile 12px in and the title 12px after it, the chips, bars and tiles drawn, the sheet's edges, the title wrapping in full, every control 44px, raw codes or paths on screen, the last card over the dock) and `.dom.json`, and `vs-proto/<state>.<theme>.<width>.pair.png` (the prototype left, the board right) for each state the prototype has. It fails on any measured fault. `DECIDE_RECORD=1` also records `decide-flow.light.390w.mp4` (`decide-record.mjs`). `DECIDE_SIDE=before SIGN_OFF_REPO=<built base checkout>` captures the base under before/.
3. Desk motion by keys: `node .agents/skills/done-done-verify-sign-off/decide-record.mjs <url> <light|dark> <out.mp4> desk` on a board `seed-board.sh` served.
4. done-done: `done-done run decide` (in `.done-done.yaml`; set `SIGN_OFF_PROTO` to the prototype's page for the pairs).
5. Journeys: `npm run build && node --test --test-global-setup=./test/temp-folder.mjs --test-name-pattern='Decide|Waiting|comment on every answer|answer events' test/design.test.mjs`.
6. Look at every still and at frames of the recording (for example `ffmpeg -i decide-flow.light.390w.mp4 -vf fps=1.4,scale=260:-1,tile=9x4 -frames:v 1 sheet.png`).

7. The leash: `LEASH_ICONS=<folder with crewhouse.svg and first-draft.svg, each project's real icon> LEASH_REVIEW_ROOT=<folder outside the run> done-done run leash` (`leash-shots.mjs`) raises on Act, then tell me, serves on When it matters until the board takes a default, and captures decide and leash (the chip's sheet) before any card is opened, then the default, agent and guard cards, then picks Ask first in the sheet and opens an ask that only waits (ask), at 360, 390, 445, 1280 and 1440 in light and dark, each with `.dom.json`, asserting the rows, icons, chip, sheet, card words and marks; at 390 light it taps Change on the agent's pick and reads the agent's wait (`wait-changed.json`, `replaces.taken: agent`). The CLI side is in `raise-act.txt`, `wait-default.txt`, `wait-agent.txt` and `answers-log.jsonl`. The paths per leash and every guard: `node --test test/leash.test.mjs`.

## Large phone boards

Decide constructs the first 24 Asks, Later and Answered rows on a phone. Show more adds the next 24 and focuses the first new ask; totals and bulk Select all include the full set. The desktop keeps every row for keyboard navigation. The extended "Decide on a phone" integration journey covers expansion, answering the last ask, bulk selection, Later and Answered expansion, and the full desktop list.

For before/after proof on a task-owned 500-card/3000-ticket loopback board, run `node .agents/skills/done-done-verify-sign-off/decide-load-shots.mjs <BASE> <OUT> before|after` through the host memory gate. It uses an isolated chrome-devtools-axi session for light/dark frames at 390 and 1280, records row/DOM counts, captures Show more and the loaded rows on phones, and closes its browser. The after journey records `show-more.mp4` with `decide-record.mjs ... load`: scroll to Show more, load, open and close a new ask. `DECIDE_PERF_BASE=<BASE> done-done run decide-load` records this in default evidence storage. BASE must be a newly seeded task board, never an installed user's board. Inspect every still and motion contact sheet; keep scratch under the run's tmp directory. The profiling journey separately captures all five Lighthouse routes.

## Gotchas

- A hash change alone keeps the page's old state; reload after changing the board through the API.
- Pointer events sent from the page (or a real CDP touch) drive the swipe; a single fast move counts as a fling.
- The page receives an item's filer as `by` (left out when it is a session id); asks carry no project, so their tile is a drawing unless the filer is a project on the board.
- Chrome under a long absolute TMPDIR can exceed its Unix socket limit: run from the evidence directory with relative `TMPDIR=tmp`, keeping scratch under that directory.
