# Decide

The Decide tab (`#waiting`) and an ask (`#decide/<key>`) at phone size first, built to match the approved feel prototype (`data/signoff-feel-v2`): list rows, an ask with nothing picked until the person picks, one button that then names the pick, Your own answer as the last card, one note, and one More, a skeleton while the board loads, a push into an ask and a pop back, a pressed row, swipes with follow-through, a toast with Undo and a countdown, the answer's check and the row folding away, an all-clear, and an approve stamp in the screen viewer. Light and dark.

## Sub-features

- Header: the date in small caps, the large title Decide, "N waiting" (with "· each has a recommended answer" when every ask to answer has one, and "· N for later"), and More (···) beside the title.
- Rows, grouped with a count each and no hairlines between them: Sign in, Answer, Review (plans, design picks and screen designs, which are looked over before they are answered) and Later. A row is a coloured squircle icon (a key, a checklist for a pick, a question for yes or no, a phone for screens), the question in up to two lines, when it was filed (today's time, Yesterday, or the date; a sign-in counts down its link in orange), and "Recommended <pick> · Agent", or what it is when it recommends nothing ("6 screens · Agent"). The board never sends who filed an ask to the page, so it says Agent where the prototype names one. A row is the link to its ask; a press tints it.
- Swipe: right answers with the recommended pick (green), left moves the ask to Later (indigo; on a Later row it brings it back). The row follows the finger; past a third of its width the action grows (a haptic tick on Android) and the swipe commits on release; a fast fling commits sooner; short of it the row springs back; past 80% it rubber-bands; a row with nothing to answer resists a right swipe. Later is kept in this browser per board file (`localStorage['sign-off-later <board file>']`); the agent still waits and the tab still counts it.
- More on the list, as the prototype has it: Answer all recommended (the Answer rows only), a gap, then Move all to later; each is greyed when it has nothing to do. Decide reads newest first, a sign-in first. Each change shows a toast with Undo.
- An ask (phone: a page of its own with a bar of back to Decide, the question once scrolled, and More; the tab bar slides away): its kind, who asked and when, the question, why, the proof it rests on as one row (a small capture, its title, Passed and when checked; a tap opens the capture in a sheet), then Your answer: the options as outlined cards with a radio, Recommended only a tag, nothing picked. The last card is Your own answer: picking it opens one box under it and the note steps aside. Under the cards one note, always open ("Note · Optional · goes with your answer"), goes with any pick. "If you stay quiet, the agent goes with <pick>." Then one button at the foot: off and reading Pick an answer until a pick, then Answer <pick>; for your own words Send answer, off until there are words. A reply ask shows only its box and Send answer. A swipe left skips to the next ask, a swipe right goes back to the list.
- Answering: the button turns green and draws a check, the row tints green and folds away, and the toast (a green check, the question in bold, the answer, "· with your comment" when a note went, Undo and a hairline counting down) rises; on a phone the list slides back in, and Undo on the list puts the row back with a blue flash and nothing picked.
- On a desk (1024px and wider) the list (head 22px down, a 28px title, rows 18px in) sits beside the selected ask (the first one on `#waiting`): a 56px bar over the ask holds its kind, who asked and when, Later (L) and More; the ask is 44px in under a 30px question, its cards numbered 1 to 9, its note open; its button shows ↵ and comes right after the ask (after the last card under a tall one, so it never covers one), with "C Note" at its right. A sign-in reads like any ask there ("Sign in to <service>", its facts, and Open sign-in with what it does beside it), with its time left only on its row; answering selects the next ask, which slides in, while the answered row folds away; the toast, 440px wide, sits centred over the list and the pane. Hovering (or focusing) a row shows its quick actions: answer with the recommended pick, and Later. Keys: J/K move, a number picks that option, Enter answers (or opens a sign-in), C goes to the note and Cmd/Ctrl+Enter answers from it, L moves to Later, E skips, Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes; all clear pops in the ask's pane.
- An ask's More holds only Ask me later (back to the list, the row moved to Later) and Dismiss, in red; a sign-in's too (the prototype shows none there, but one started on this host never expires, and Dismiss clears it). The next ask is a swipe left or E.
- A sign-in, on a phone a page of its own: its key large in orange, "Sign in · <who waits> · <time>", "Sign in to <service>", why it waits, then Unlocks, Expires in (a clock counting down each second) and Where as one inset group, and Open sign-in with "Opens <service> in a new tab. This card closes itself once you are in." under it; once tapped the button spins and reads Waiting for you to sign in.
- Every toast has one style: a coloured disc with its mark (a check for a save, a clock for Later, an arrow for an undo, a tray for a dismiss, a stop for a failure), the words, Undo where it applies, and a countdown hairline.
- First load: shimmering rows hold the place until the board file is read; the rows then rise in, a little apart.
- All clear: once nothing waits, a green check pops in over "All clear" and "Nothing is waiting on you, <name>." (a desk's pane adds "Agents will ask when they need you.").
- Screen viewer (`#designs/<key>`, Review full screen): an approved screen carries a green check stamp in its corner, which springs in as the approval saves.
- Reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`): no slides, rises, pops or springs; every state change still happens.

## Driving it

1. `npm run build`.
2. Stills, 390 and 1280 (`DECIDE_WIDTHS`), light and dark, on a fresh seeded board it serves and stops itself: `.agents/skills/done-done-verify-sign-off/decide-shots.sh <folder>` writes decide, decide-signin (the npm sign-in), decide-item, decide-item-end (the ask scrolled to its bar), decide-answered, decide-undo-toast and, on this build, decide-press, decide-swipe-right, decide-swipe-left, decide-menu, decide-item-more, decide-swiped-answer and decide-all-clear (`<state>.<theme>.<width>w.png`), at 1280 also decide-keys (the ? sheet) and decide-hover (a row's quick actions), and steps.json. Run the same script from a base checkout's build into before/: the shared names pair up.
3. Beside the prototype: `PROTO_SHOTS=<the prototype's shots folder> MATCH_RECORD=1 .agents/skills/done-done-verify-sign-off/decide-match.sh <folder>` seeds the prototype's own demo data (`seed-proto-board.sh`), captures every Decide state the prototype has a shot of (decide, decide-menu, decide-press, both swipes, pick, pick-own, pick-comment, pick-proof, pick-toast, signin, signin-wait, decide-empty at 390; desk-decide, desk-menu, desk-pick, desk-pick-option, desk-pick-comment, desk-answered, desk-later, desk-keys, desk-allclear at 1280) in light and dark, writes each pair to `side/` (the prototype left, the board right, the same size), and records `decide-flows.<theme>.390w.mp4` (a pick with a note, Your own answer, a pick with no note and its Undo, More on the list, an ask's More and Ask me later).
4. Motion: seed a board (`d=$(mktemp -d); url=$(.agents/skills/done-done-verify-sign-off/seed-board.sh "$d" | tail -1)`), then `node .agents/skills/done-done-verify-sign-off/decide-record.mjs "$url" <light|dark> <out.mp4>` (its own headless Chromium; the first board read is held 1.2 s so the skeleton shows), or add `desk` for the desk by keys at 1280 (a pill names each key), then stop the board as SKILL.md says. `DECIDE_RECORD=1 decide-shots.sh` records both.
5. done-done: `done-done run decide` (in `.done-done.yaml`) runs the stills at 390 and 1280 and the recording into one run folder with its step record, for `done-done check` and the merge card.
6. Journeys: `npm run build && node --test --test-global-setup=./test/temp-folder.mjs --test-name-pattern='Decide|Waiting|comment on every answer' test/design.test.mjs` drives a real finger swipe right (answer, Undo) and left (Later, kept across a reload, back), the push, nothing picked until a pick, the button naming it, Your own answer's box and Send answer, the note sent with a pick, More holding only Ask me later and Dismiss, the check and fold, Undo in place, More's Move all to later, and the plan row; on a desk, the list beside its ask, a sign-in laid out as an ask with no second countdown, a six-option ask whose bar stays under its last card, J and K, a number then Enter, Cmd+Z, L, C with Cmd+Enter, a row's hover answer, the ? sheet, G then a letter, and the phone layout under 1024px.
7. Look at every still and at frames of each recording (for example `ffmpeg -i decide.light.390w.mp4 -vf fps=1.4,scale=260:-1,tile=9x4 -frames:v 1 sheet.png`).

## Gotchas

- A hash change alone keeps the page's old state; reload after changing the board through the API.
- Pointer events sent from the page (or a real CDP touch) drive the swipe; a single fast move counts as a fling.
- The push slides the page's content, not the dock, so the dock never leaves the screen mid-slide.
