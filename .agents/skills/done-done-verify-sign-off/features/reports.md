# Reports

The Reports tab (`#reports`), as the approved new-look prototype draws it (signoff-restyle-board `proto/index.html`, frames `v2/review/phone/reports*.png` and `report.*.png`): what agents filed to explain something.

## Sub-features

- Head: the title Reports in the 44px title row with the shared round 40px light-or-dark button at its right (a moon in light, a sun in dark; the pick is kept in this browser, as on Today), then Newest or By project (a 40px segmented control; the choice is kept in this browser), then Still to read: how many listed are not read yet "of" all listed, and how many reports were filed this week (archived ones too).
- List, Newest: one card of rows, newest first, six of them with See all (Show fewer) for the rest. By project: one card per project under its name and count, the most reports first and No project last. On a desk every row stays, for J and K.
- Row (ui.css's shared row, 68px on a phone): the project's own icon (`sign-off-axi project --name <name> --icon <path>`, served from the board's folder), else its initials, else the page drawing for a report with no project; the title in plain words (no ticket code such as "6198 RL-02:" ahead of it, no file path in it), a P0 to P3 chip only when the report has a priority; then, on every row as the prototype draws it, a bar of how much of it is read with its percent: 100% once read, a guided report not read yet by its parts read (40% for two of five), else 0%. On a desk a long title takes two lines.
- Read state, kept beside the board (`<board>.reads.jsonl`, a `{key, read}` line per mark, the last one wins), so a phone and a desk agree: a report filed today is new until it is opened; opening it reads it. A guided report's parts read (`{key, part}` lines) fill its bar while it is still new. The dock's Reports carries the count of what is new; the desk sidebar counts the same.
- Swipes (phone and desk, by pointer): right toggles read and unread, left archives it with Undo; the action under the row is ink.
- One report on a phone: a sheet over the list (a handle, a 28px top corner): its project's mark, what it is (Guided report, Before and after, Chart, Diagram, or Report) and the day, with a round Close; the title in full; what it says; What it found (a guided report's headline) or What it decided in a sunk panel; its ticket and Activity; then the page in its frame, or for a guided report tapped in the list "Read k of n parts" with a check on each part read and Read the report, both opening the guided page in full (a part at that part). A guided report reached by a link (Today's card, the CLI's open) opens in full at once. Dismiss sits at the foot. A page report's sheet holds its top 48px down so nothing moves while the page loads.
- Notes (n) and Archived (n) with Restore stay at the end of the list, then More as one quiet line naming what it holds (as on Today), opening a sheet with Mark all as read (while one is new), Select and Archive all. The end of the list clears the dock.
- Desk (1024px and up, on the desk shell, board-shell.md): the same rows in the list column (the order in the shell's head) beside the report shown as the prototype's open report ([Card detail](card-detail.md)): the newest shown and not yet read until a row is picked, J and K, U (Mark read or unread), E (Archive) with Cmd/Ctrl+Z, hover Read and Archive, and a guided report's Read the report (Enter) opening over the board, Escape back.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open the board, tap Reports in the dock (phone) or the sidebar (desk).

## Driving it

`node .agents/skills/done-done-verify-sign-off/reports-shots.mjs <folder> [<prototype index.html>]` (journey `reports`; `REPORTS_WIDTHS=390` keeps one width; `REPORTS_PROTO` names the prototype too). It seeds a fresh board with eight reports filed in the last day in five projects plus one with none (Beacon and Settings with their own icon, two with a priority, one titled with a ticket code and a file path in what it says), three read and a guided report with two of its five parts read (it names ticket T-29, which is on the board, and carries one comment), then drives its own headless Chromium over the DevTools protocol at exactly 390x844 and 1280x800 (2x), light and dark. At 390 it captures `reports`, `reports-all` (See all), `reports-project`, `report-guided` (tapped in the list), `report-guided-end` (its sheet scrolled to the ticket row and Activity), `report-page`, `reports-swipe-right`, `reports-archived`, `reports-menu` (the More sheet) and `reports-toggled` (the other theme picked with the button); at 1280 `reports-desk`, `reports-desk-next` (after J and U) and `reports-guided-open`. Next to each measured capture it writes `<state>.<cell>.measure.json` and `.dom.json`, and `reports.<cell>.txt`: the head, each row (icon or initials, chip, its percent), the edges (16 and 374 at 390) and the 4px grid, rows 68px with a 40px tile, the second line 4px under the title and the 40px percent column, a helper line or title cut short, raw codes or paths in the text, the end of the list against the dock, the light-or-dark button (40px, on the right edge, moon or sun), the sheet's title, panels and parts, every control against 44px, overflow and console errors, and it asserts each: Newest shows six of eight, Still to read 5 of 8, every row's bar matching its percent, By project's order, the guided sheet's 2 of 5 parts, its ticket row and Activity, the page in the page sheet, a guided report by its link opening in full and Read the report doing so from the sheet (its row then 100%), a swipe right marking unread, a swipe left archiving with Undo, the More sheet's items, the button switching the theme (its icon and the canvas), and on a desk a title on two lines, J reading the next report and U marking it unread. With the prototype's page, each phone state is also captured from the prototype at the same size and theme and saved beside the board's (`vs-proto/<state>.<cell>.pair.png`). With `REPORTS_RECORD=1` (as the journey runs) it records `reports.<theme>.390w.mp4` (`decide-record.mjs <url> <theme> <out> reports`).

Board actions (`board-actions.mjs`) clicks the Reports actions in every cell: a swipe right to mark unread, Mark all as read with Undo, open, Select, Dismiss 1 with Undo, Cancel and Archive all with Undo, and on a report Close and Dismiss (phone) or Mark unread and Archive (desk).

Regression: test/design.test.mjs ("Reports on a desk") drives the desk at 1280 by keys and checks the phone layout at 1000px; ("each card has one place to type ... a report opens full screen and back") checks the head, the hero, the row, the swipe layers and the count, then at 390 opens a report in its sheet (read saved, Read and the count gone), closes it, swipes right (unread saved, New back) and left (archived, Undo restores); ("report and plan documents use one page scroll") checks the sheet is the page's one scroll.
