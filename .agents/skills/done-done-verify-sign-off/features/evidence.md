# Evidence

The Evidence area (`#evidence`) in the Spacious look: every proof ever filed in one list, newest first, with no project or day headings, the capture as the card's hero, and a search box over it with a quiet project picker beside it when proof names more than one project. An update is never an ask: each card says its honest result and only a passed run shows the tick. A card opens the update's own screen showing the whole capture, what it proves, the result, what ran, the change, who checked it and when.

## Sub-features

- Evidence lists every proof that is not dismissed (`evidenceCard.result` is `RUN PASS`, or the update has no run to check and says `Not run`, or its run `Could not check`), newest first, however old, the capture as the hero. A `Failed` or `Did not finish` run is no proof and is not listed; none counts toward Waiting on you, the Decide badge or the Need you tile; its own screen (`#evidence/<key>`) shows its result, the reason a run could not be checked and the normal verdicts and Dismiss. Only Today's Proven today row, tile and glance line are limited to passed updates filed in the last 24 hours. The search box above the list narrows it by title, note, project and proof line. The picker (`#ev-project`, **All projects** first) names each project in the run's proof line (`[done-done-]verify-<project>: ...`), trimmed and compared without case so `verify-Sign-Off:` and `done-done-verify-sign-off:` are one choice; proof with no such line takes the repository of its change link; one with neither is **Other**, last. With one project the picker is not shown. Picking a project narrows the list, still newest first; search applies on top. Each card shows its first picture (or clip) as the hero, filling a tile half as tall as it is wide from the top of the shot, the title, its result and its filing day and time (`Today, 08:35`); proof with neither (a run that kept only text) has no tile, just the title and result. Cards listed together under the same headline (two runs proving one outcome) each name, under it, what tells them apart best: the project, the update's own title or what ran; Proven today does the same over its three. A capture that changed or went missing says so instead of showing a broken frame.
- The card opens `#evidence/<key>`. The item shows every capture of the update, the title, the one-line about, `Proof` rows for Result, What ran, Checked by, Project, When and a Change link to the pull request, then the verdicts (Pass, Fail and Needs work on one row of equal buttons, at every width) and the raw run lines under Details. From 64rem the capture fills the preview's width, a tall phone shot cropped to the top in a 4:3 frame with an **Open full size** label; a tap opens the file full size in a new tab. Checked by is the one-word checker named before the first colon of the run's `proof` line (for example `done-done-verify-sign-off`); What ran is the rest of that line. A proof line with no such prefix shows no Checked by row and What ran is the whole line.
- From 64rem (1024px) Evidence is a list beside the selected update, as in Mail, on the same desk shell as Decide (`split()`): each card is a row (a small capture, the title, its result and time) in one column that scrolls on its own, and the update opens to its right. With no update in the address the newest is selected. The selected row is tinted with an accent bar; a click, J or K moves the selection and the update slides in from the side it moved (no slide with reduced motion). A row's × shows on hover or focus. Below 64rem it is the phone layout: cards keep the screen's width with the whole capture inside the card, and a card opens the update on its own screen.
- From 64rem Today is a dashboard: Waiting on you on the left; Proven today (stacked rows, the same row as Evidence's) and the new report (a small picture beside its words) in a column on its right.
- Nothing on Evidence archives by age: an update filed a week ago is still listed and `sign-off-axi list --full` says `shown` for it. Reports still archive after a day under `Archived (N)`. The same filer posting an evidence message again under its key with a changed proof replaces the card (one card, filed now, no `.dismissed.jsonl` line); another filer, or an answered card, is refused with exit 2.
- A verdict saved from the item stays on that screen; back on the list a passed update stays on Evidence and in Proven today, and also shows under Answered.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open the URL seed-board.sh prints, then tap Evidence in the sidebar or the tab bar. Tap a card to open the update; the ‹ Evidence link returns to the list.

## Driving it

1. Launch as SKILL.md says and keep BASE.
`done-done run today-evidence` seeds passed, expired and no-run updates across two projects (sign-off and muxr) and one with none, captures Today at 390 and Evidence at 390 and 1280 in both themes, the passed update `evidence-item.<theme>.<width>w.png` and Today's closed Ask the agent `today-ask.<theme>.<width>w.png` at 390 and 1280, one crop per claim at 390 and 1280 (`preview-fill` at 1280: the capture across its preview with Open full size; `verdicts`: the one-row Pass, Fail and Needs work; `ask-line`: the closed Ask the agent line under the Leash; `ev-tile` at 390: one card's filled tile; `twins`: the two same-headline cards with their telling lines), each shot settled and cut from the viewport to the elements' box, and Evidence with muxr picked (`project-filter.<theme>.390w.png`). `counts.txt` says how many headings sit inside the list (0), whether the cards are newest first by their filed time (true), the picker's choices and how many cards show with muxr picked (1).
2. Capture the list at both widths and themes: `shots.sh BASE "$DONE_DONE_EVIDENCE_ROOT" evidence`.
3. The seed links one update to a passing done-done run and leaves another plain, so the list shows the `Passed` card first and no project picker (one project only); the plain update (`Not run`) is on Evidence but not in Proven today, and the Need you tile and Decide badge count neither. Today's Proven today row and Evidence name the same cards in the same order. Open the passed update, `BASE#evidence/update-2`, and capture it at both widths and themes as `evidence-item.<theme>.<width>w.png`; it shows the Result, What ran, Checked by, Project, When and Change rows.
4. On a phone, check the card fills the content width and the item never scrolls sideways; long values such as `done-done-verify-sign-off` must fit, moving below their label when needed. At 1280, `counts.txt` says the list is one column, the newest is selected and open beside it, and Proven today sits right of Waiting on you, how many cards name what differs from a twin, the update's capture width against the preview, the rows the verdicts take (1) and the closed Ask the agent's height and border (44 px, 0px); `TODAY_EVIDENCE_RECORD=1` records the hover, press and selection (`desk-evidence.<theme>.1280w.mp4`, by `desk-evidence-record.mjs`). `done-done run board-actions` checks the seeded long checker value at 390 and 1280 in both themes.
5. Save a verdict from the item and confirm the update stays on Evidence and in Proven today and the glance line on Today; undo it if the board is reused. In a terminal, `post --title "Does it pass?" --option pass=Pass --option fail=Fail` is refused (exit 2) pointing to `--type evidence`, and `refile <key> --media shot.png` turns an old waiting Pass/Fail ask into an update and dismisses the ask.
6. Keep all: stop the board, set `filed` of `update-1` in the seed's `board.json` to two days ago, serve it again and capture `#evidence` at 390 and 1280: the update is still listed, last, its time saying the day it was filed, and no `Archived` row appears. Repost the contract evidence message (`post --kind evidence --from evidence-run.json --filer verify`) with a changed title: one card remains, `board.dismissed.jsonl` does not exist. Then `sign-off-axi post` a second Yes/No ask whose title repeats an open one (refused, exit 1, the refusal names the open card), answer the first and confirm `list` shows the twin answered too, and run `sign-off-axi dismiss --filer verify` to see that filer's cards leave Decide.
7. Look at every capture: the capture is whole inside its tile, nothing is cut and the tab bar covers no text.
