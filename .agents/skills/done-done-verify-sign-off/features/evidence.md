# Evidence

The Evidence area (`#evidence`) in the Spacious look: every passed update filed in the last day, grouped by the project named in the check that ran, with the capture as the card's hero. An update is never an ask: each card says its honest result and only a passed run shows the tick. A card opens the update's own screen showing the whole capture, what it proves, the result, what ran, the change, who checked it and when.

## Sub-features

- Evidence lists passed proof only (`evidenceCard.result` is `RUN PASS`, filed in the last 24 hours, not dismissed), newest first, the capture as the hero, each with the tick and `Passed`. It is the very list Proven today, its count and the glance line on Today read, so the two always agree. A capture with no linked run or a run found nowhere (a board with no `--evidence-root` finds a run in `~/.done-done/evidence/<app>/.done-done/runs`), and a `Failed`, `Did not finish` or `Could not check` run, is no proof: it is on neither list, and none counts toward Waiting on you, the Decide badge or the Need you tile; its own screen (`#evidence/<key>`) shows its result, the reason a run could not be checked and the normal verdicts and Dismiss. Updates are grouped by the project in their run's proof line (`[done-done-]verify-<project>: ...`), trimmed and compared without case so `verify-Sign-Off:` and `done-done-verify-sign-off:` share one head; proof with no such line takes the repository of its change link; one with neither goes under **Other**, last. Each card shows its first picture (or clip) as the hero, the title, its result and the filing time. A capture that changed or went missing says so instead of showing a broken frame.
- The card opens `#evidence/<key>`. The item shows every capture of the update, the title, the one-line about, `Proof` rows for Result, What ran, Checked by, Project, When and a Change link to the pull request, then the verdicts and the raw run lines under Details. Checked by is the one-word checker named before the first colon of the run's `proof` line (for example `done-done-verify-sign-off`); What ran is the rest of that line. A proof line with no such prefix shows no Checked by row and What ran is the whole line.
- Select and Clear all sit in the title bar at the right (44px tall, above the large title, not a row of their own); each project head is a small uppercase label (12px) over its cards, in light and dark with the same spacing.
- On a desk (60rem and wider) cards sit two across. On a phone they keep the screen's width with the whole capture inside the card.
- An update filed over 24 hours ago leaves the list by itself and waits under `Archived (N)` at the end of Evidence (Reports has the same for what only reads): nothing is deleted, `Restore` brings it back and a log line keeps it there, and `sign-off-axi list --full` says `dismissed` for it. A read-only board archives nothing.
- A verdict saved from the item stays on that screen; back on the list a passed update stays on Evidence and in Proven today, and also shows under Answered.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open the URL seed-board.sh prints, then tap Evidence in the sidebar or the tab bar. Tap a card to open the update; the ‹ Evidence link returns to the list.

## Driving it

1. Launch as SKILL.md says and keep BASE.
`done-done run today-evidence` seeds passed, expired and no-run updates and captures Today and Evidence at 390 in both themes: both show the same passed cards.
2. Capture the list at both widths and themes: `shots.sh BASE "$DONE_DONE_EVIDENCE_ROOT" evidence`.
3. The seed links one update to a passing done-done run and leaves another plain, so the list shows the `sign-off` project head with the `Passed` card only; the plain update (`Not run`) is on neither Evidence nor Proven today, and the Need you tile and Decide badge count neither. Today's Proven today row and Evidence name the same cards in the same order. Open the passed update, `BASE#evidence/update-2`, and capture it at both widths and themes as `evidence-item.<theme>.<width>w.png`; it shows the Result, What ran, Checked by, Project, When and Change rows.
4. On a phone, check the card fills the content width and the item never scrolls sideways; long values such as `done-done-verify-sign-off` must fit, moving below their label when needed. On a desk, check cards sit two across. `done-done run board-actions` checks the seeded long checker value at 390 and 1280 in both themes.
5. Save a verdict from the item and confirm the update stays on Evidence and in Proven today and the glance line on Today; undo it if the board is reused. In a terminal, `post --title "Does it pass?" --option pass=Pass --option fail=Fail` is refused (exit 2) pointing to `--type evidence`, and `refile <key> --media shot.png` turns an old waiting Pass/Fail ask into an update and dismisses the ask.
6. Archive: stop the board, set `filed` of `update-1` in the seed's `board.json` to two days ago, serve it again and capture `#evidence` at 390 and 1280: the update is gone from the list and `Archived (1)` sits under it; open the fold, click `Restore` and capture the list with the update back. Then `sign-off-axi post` a second Yes/No ask whose title repeats an open one (refused, exit 1, the refusal names the open card), answer the first and confirm `list` shows the twin answered too, and run `sign-off-axi dismiss --filer verify` to see that filer's cards leave Decide.
7. Look at every capture: the capture is whole inside its tile, nothing is cut and the tab bar covers no text.
