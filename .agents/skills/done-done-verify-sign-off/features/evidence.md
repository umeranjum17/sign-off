# Evidence

The Evidence area (`#evidence`) in the approved new look: the title row with a round project filter, Newest and By priority, **Held up today** (how many of the day's proofs done-done says hold up, how many are not checked, and one square per proof filed today in the order filed: ink where it holds up, red where it failed or could not be confirmed), then the proof as rows, six until **See all**. A row is its project's icon tile (the icon the board set with `sign-off-axi project --icon`, else the shared project outline) with done-done's check at its corner, the title, a P0-P3 chip only where the card has a priority, and its ticket's bar and percent once work on the ticket has begun (a full bar at 100%), else its project and time. A proof opens as a sheet over the list on a phone (beside it on a desk): its project and time, its whole title, the capture, done-done's word, Priority, Ticket and Pull request rows, what it says, its Activity, then Fail, Needs work and Pass. An update is never an ask.

## Sub-features

- Evidence lists every proof that is not dismissed (`evidenceCard.result` is `RUN PASS`, or the update has no run to check, or its run `Could not check`), newest first, however old. A `Failed` or `Did not finish` run is no proof and is not listed; none counts toward Waiting on you, the Decide badge or the Answer, Review and Sign in tiles; its own screen (`#evidence/<key>`) shows done-done's word (Failed, Could not check with the reason and the next step), the verdicts and Dismiss. Today's Proofs today tile counts the updates filed today, the failed runs too, and opens this list.
- done-done's word: the board's check of the linked run when it has one (`RUN PASS` holds up), else the judge's line at the start of the update's `about` ("done-done checked the proof and it holds up", "done-done could not confirm the proof"), as the fleet's board, served with no evidence root, keeps it. Nothing else is Not checked. The line itself is not repeated under the title; what follows it is.
- Held up today counts every proof filed today (listed or not); with none it says None filed yet. Its squares fill the card's width in 24 to 30 columns, the box rounded up to the 4px grid. It is hidden while one project is picked.
- Newest keeps the list's order; By priority puts P0 first, then P1 to P3, then the rest, newest first inside each. The section head says Latest proof or By priority, with See all (Show fewer) past six.
- The project filter (`.ev-fb`, shown only when proof names more than one project) opens a sheet of each project with its icon and count (most first, No project last) and a check on the one shown; a pick shows a pill (`.ev-pill`) with the project's tile and an x that clears it. The project is the one in the run's proof line (`[done-done-]verify-<project>: ...`), compared without case, else the repository of the change link. There is no search box on Evidence: the dock's Search finds proof with everything else.
- Owner-facing words carry no file paths or ticket codes: a path in what an update says reads "the file" (or "the report", "the plan"), and a leading "6198 ", "P0 " or "XX-12:" leaves the title.
- The proof's sheet on a phone sits 48px down with a handle, the list dimmed behind it and the dock under it; its x (or Escape) closes it back to the list. Its capture fills the sheet's width (up to 420px tall, whole), a tap on a desk opens the file full size; a proof with no picture says Text only: no capture was filed. The Ticket row opens the ticket (a bar once begun, full at 100%; none at 0%); the Pull request row opens the change. Its Activity lists its posting, a comment sent from the sheet, the answer and the agent picking it up. Fail, Needs work and Pass sit on one row, Pass in ink until a verdict is saved, then the saved one; Add a comment and Dismiss follow.
- The title row carries the shared light-or-dark button (Today's, `.ui-rb` labelled Switch to dark or light): a moon in light, a sun in dark; a tap turns the board to the other theme and keeps the pick in this browser (`so-theme`).
- Select and Clear all stay reachable: one quiet line under the list (`.ev-more`, "Select or clear all") opens them in a sheet; Select puts Cancel and Dismiss N under the title, a tap on a row picks it, and Dismiss archives the picked proof with Undo; Clear all archives every listed proof with Undo.
- From 64rem Today sets Up next and its two tiles in a 400px column beside Waiting on you (features/today.md).
- From 64rem Evidence is the same list in the desk shell's four columns (board-shell.md), with its order and filter in the shell's head, beside the selected proof (the newest with none in the address), the row shown on the sunk fill; J and K move it. A desk row's title wraps to two lines. The proof opens as the prototype's open proof ([Card detail](card-detail.md), `proofPane`): its title and capture beside its panel, what it says, the verdict and its Activity.
- Nothing on Evidence archives by age: an update filed a week ago is still listed and `sign-off-axi list --full` says `shown` for it. The same filer posting an evidence message again under its key with a changed proof replaces the card (one card, filed now, no `.dismissed.jsonl` line); another filer, or an answered card, is refused with exit 2.
- Light pictures: `post` keeps every still as posted and makes beside it a light copy (at most 1600 pixels wide, `<n>-light.jpg` or `.png`) and a thumb (up to 720 by 1440, cropped to at most 2:1 from the top or 1:2 from the centre, `<n>-thumb.*`), each only when it weighs less than what it stands in for. The proof's sheet loads the light copy, Decide the thumb, lazily; Open full size opens the original.
- Load: the list carries no captures (only project icons), so it stays light however long it is; a capture's address carries the first 16 hex of its sha256 (`&h=`) and is kept for good (`immutable`); the page, the state and every other file carry an ETag, so a reload and the 20-second poll of an unchanged board are 304s; text goes brotli or gzip. `load-perf.mjs` measures a real board; the `load-budget` journey guards the bytes and requests.
- A verdict saved from the sheet stays on it; back on the list a passed update stays on Evidence and in Today's Proofs today count, and also shows under Answered.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open the URL seed-board.sh prints, then tap Evidence in the sidebar or the tab bar. Tap a card to open the update; the ‹ Evidence link returns to the list.

## Driving it

1. The new look beside the approved prototype: `EVIDENCE_PROTO=<the prototype's index.html> done-done run evidence-newlook` (or `node .agents/skills/done-done-verify-sign-off/evidence-shots.mjs <folder> <the prototype's index.html>`) fills a throwaway board with the prototype's own proof (its 40 newest proofs with their captures, projects and icons, priorities and tickets, and the day's earlier proofs, archived, for Held up today), serves it with no evidence root, and captures at 390x844 (2x) in light and dark: `evidence`, `evidence-priority`, `filter`, `evidence-crewhouse`, `evidence-all` (See all, at its end), `evidence-end-iphone` (the same end with an iPhone's 34px home bar, set over the DevTools protocol, so the dock rises 34px), `proof` and `proof-end` (its Activity with a comment sent from the sheet, and the verdicts), plus `evidence` and `evidence-priority` at 1280x800. Each phone state the prototype has a frame of is saved beside it (`vs-proto/<state>.<theme>.390w.pair.png`, the prototype left). `measure.txt` and each `.measure.json` hold what the browser measured: edges 16-374, the 4px grid, 68px rows with 40px tiles 12px in, the chip and the percent on one right edge, square dots, the last row against the dock, the last row and Archived above the dock at the scroll end, no picture or close on a row, the proof's Close above its captures and over none, the sheet's top (48) and whole title, the verdicts on one row, file paths shown (none) and controls under 44px (none); the run fails on any.
2. `done-done run today-evidence` seeds passed, expired and no-run updates across two projects (sign-off and muxr) and one with none, captures Today at 390 and Evidence at 390 and 1280 in both themes, the passed update `evidence-item.<theme>.<width>w.png`, one crop per claim (`preview-fill` at 1280, `verdicts`, `ev-row` at 390), the old and reposted proof found with See all, and Evidence with muxr picked (`project-filter.<theme>.390w.png`). `counts.txt` says how many headings sit inside the list (0), whether the rows are newest first (true), the filter's projects and how many rows show with muxr picked (1).
3. Save a verdict from the sheet and confirm the update stays on Evidence and in Today's Proofs today count. In a terminal, `post --title "Does it pass?" --option pass=Pass --option fail=Fail` is refused (exit 2) pointing to `--type evidence`.
4. Keep all: set `filed` of an update in the seed's `board.json` to two days ago, serve it again: the update is still listed (See all), its time saying the day it was filed, and no `Archived` row appears.
5. Light pictures: `IMAGES_DIR=<folder of real captures> done-done run images` posts them and saves the update and decide shots with a `.json` of every picture shown; every picture's URL ends `&v=light` or `&v=thumb`.
6. Look at every capture: nothing is cut, the dock covers no text, the sheet's title is whole, no path or ticket code shows.
7. The regression: test/design.test.mjs ("Evidence: a row per proof") opens the board at 390x844 with the 34px home bar, checks that no row carries a picture or a close and that the proof's Close ends above its captures, then dismisses the proof from its sheet and checks that at the scroll end the last row, Select or clear all and Archived (1) end above the dock.
