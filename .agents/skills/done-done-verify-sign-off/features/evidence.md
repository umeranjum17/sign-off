# Evidence

The Evidence area (`#evidence`) in the Spacious look: every proof ever filed, newest first, grouped by the project named in the check that ran and then by the day it was filed, with the capture as the card's hero and a search box over it. An update is never an ask: each card says its honest result and only a passed run shows the tick. A card opens the update's own screen showing the whole capture, what it proves, the result, what ran, the change, who checked it and when.

## Sub-features

- Evidence lists every proof that is not dismissed (`evidenceCard.result` is `RUN PASS`, or the update has no run to check and says `Not run`, or its run `Could not check`), newest first, however old, the capture as the hero. A `Failed` or `Did not finish` run is no proof and is not listed; none counts toward Waiting on you, the Decide badge or the Need you tile; its own screen (`#evidence/<key>`) shows its result, the reason a run could not be checked and the normal verdicts and Dismiss. Only Today's Proven today row, tile and glance line are limited to passed updates filed in the last 24 hours. The search box above the list narrows it by title, note, project and proof line. Updates are grouped by the project in their run's proof line (`[done-done-]verify-<project>: ...`), trimmed and compared without case so `verify-Sign-Off:` and `done-done-verify-sign-off:` share one head; proof with no such line takes the repository of its change link; one with neither goes under **Other**, last. Inside a project, cards sit under a day heading (`Today`, then `Monday 19 October`). Each card shows its first picture (or clip) as the hero, the title, its result and the filing time. A capture that changed or went missing says so instead of showing a broken frame.
- The card opens `#evidence/<key>`. The item shows every capture of the update, the title, the one-line about, `Proof` rows for Result, What ran, Checked by, Project, When and a Change link to the pull request, then the verdicts and the raw run lines under Details. Checked by is the one-word checker named before the first colon of the run's `proof` line (for example `done-done-verify-sign-off`); What ran is the rest of that line. A proof line with no such prefix shows no Checked by row and What ran is the whole line.
- On a desk (60rem and wider) cards sit two across. On a phone they keep the screen's width with the whole capture inside the card.
- Nothing on Evidence archives by age: an update filed a week ago is still listed and `sign-off-axi list --full` says `shown` for it. Reports still archive after a day under `Archived (N)`. The same filer posting an evidence message again under its key with a changed proof replaces the card (one card, filed now, no `.dismissed.jsonl` line); another filer, or an answered card, is refused with exit 2.
- A verdict saved from the item stays on that screen; back on the list a passed update stays on Evidence and in Proven today, and also shows under Answered.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open the URL seed-board.sh prints, then tap Evidence in the sidebar or the tab bar. Tap a card to open the update; the ‹ Evidence link returns to the list.

## Driving it

1. Launch as SKILL.md says and keep BASE.
`done-done run today-evidence` seeds passed, expired and no-run updates and captures Today and Evidence at 390 in both themes: both show the same passed cards.
2. Capture the list at both widths and themes: `shots.sh BASE "$DONE_DONE_EVIDENCE_ROOT" evidence`.
3. The seed links one update to a passing done-done run and leaves another plain, so the list shows the `sign-off` project head with the `Passed` card only; the plain update (`Not run`) is on Evidence but not in Proven today, and the Need you tile and Decide badge count neither. Today's Proven today row and Evidence name the same cards in the same order. Open the passed update, `BASE#evidence/update-2`, and capture it at both widths and themes as `evidence-item.<theme>.<width>w.png`; it shows the Result, What ran, Checked by, Project, When and Change rows.
4. On a phone, check the card fills the content width and the item never scrolls sideways; long values such as `done-done-verify-sign-off` must fit, moving below their label when needed. On a desk, check cards sit two across. `done-done run board-actions` checks the seeded long checker value at 390 and 1280 in both themes.
5. Save a verdict from the item and confirm the update stays on Evidence and in Proven today and the glance line on Today; undo it if the board is reused. In a terminal, `post --title "Does it pass?" --option pass=Pass --option fail=Fail` is refused (exit 2) pointing to `--type evidence`, and `refile <key> --media shot.png` turns an old waiting Pass/Fail ask into an update and dismisses the ask.
6. Keep all: stop the board, set `filed` of `update-1` in the seed's `board.json` to two days ago, serve it again and capture `#evidence` at 390 and 1280: the update is still listed under its own day heading and no `Archived` row appears. Repost the contract evidence message (`post --kind evidence --from evidence-run.json --filer verify`) with a changed title: one card remains, `board.dismissed.jsonl` does not exist. Then `sign-off-axi post` a second Yes/No ask whose title repeats an open one (refused, exit 1, the refusal names the open card), answer the first and confirm `list` shows the twin answered too, and run `sign-off-axi dismiss --filer verify` to see that filer's cards leave Decide.
7. Look at every capture: the capture is whole inside its tile, nothing is cut and the tab bar covers no text.
