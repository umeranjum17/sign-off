# Inbox

One more menu beside Today, Decide, Evidence, Reports and Tickets (dock and sidebar; address `#inbox`): the catch-up on every conversation with an agent or lead, whatever its kind (decision, plan, report, design, ticket, proof), one thread per agent and per topic (below), each row showing where that thread stands now (new reply, waiting on you, waiting on an agent, done). It does not replace any of those menus, and Today stays the first screen.

## What it shows

- The list, in four sections: **New** (a reply not opened yet, and a report or proof not read), **Decide** (what waits on the person), **Waiting on agents** (each row says what the person sent and its step: Sent, Delivered, Read), and **Done**, folded until the Done entry at the rail's foot is tapped. A thread is Done once its question is answered and acted on, its work is merged or it is closed; it stays in search, and returns to the live list when a new message arrives on it. Newest first in each; eight rows until `Show N more`.
- Each row says where its thread stands on both sides: a count of the agent's replies not opened yet, and a quiet **Not read** mark when something the person sent has not been read by the agent (a send with no read mark and no reply). Two filters under the kind chips, **Unread from agents** and **Not read by agents**, each count the threads they list and narrow the rail; a second tap lets one go. The Inbox badge is unchanged: only what needs the person.
- The rail ends on two quiet entries: **Agents** (read-only, the Channels room) and **Done**, which holds the threads the person marked Done and the ones that finished on their own (answered and acted on, merged or closed; the old Archived). A row's own **Done** control, or a swipe left on the row, marks its thread Done: it leaves the live rail at once, a toast with **Undo** stays up for six seconds, and nothing is deleted. The mark is kept beside the board, in the file that holds what was read, never on a card, so it is still Done after a reload. Picked, **Done** is a view of those threads alone, and the search reads them there; a Done thread opens as the same chat. A thread something lands on after it was marked Done (its agent's next words, a new card) is back in the live rail, unread, its count cleared when it is opened. On a desk the rail is about 19rem beside the one open conversation; on a phone it is the Inbox screen and a thread is one screen over it.
- A search box and kind chips (All, Decisions, Plans, Reports, Designs, Tickets, Proofs; a kind shows only when the board holds one, with what is still open). Tickets reads the board's ticket roll-up when picked. The chips and the two unread filters are one row that scrolls sideways, never wrapping; on a phone Agents and Done are one short row right under it, so both are on the first screen.
- The Inbox tab and sidebar entry carry a red count of New and Decide threads.
- **One thread per agent and per topic.** The agent is the card's filer (the card's `by` on the board); the topic is the card's ticket when it has one, else the card itself. An agent's plan, proof and report on one ticket share a thread, a lone ask is its own, and another agent's card on the same ticket is a thread of its own. The thread is named by the ticket's title, else its first card's title, with the agent as a small label above the name (and `N items` when it holds more than one card); the person cannot rename it, so the same topic always finds the same thread. A thread exists once the person took part in one of its cards; the other cards then sit in it. Nothing new is stored: threads are read from the cards, the trail, comments, tickets and dismissals the board already keeps. A thread's state is its most urgent card's (New, Decide, Waiting on agents, Done), its counts are the sums over its cards, and the Inbox badge counts threads. The thread's address is its oldest card's key (`#inbox/<key>`); any of its cards' keys opens the same thread, and `/open` on a card's key opens that card.
- A row opens its thread (`#inbox/<key>`; a ticket is `#inbox/~<id>`) as a chat, in time order: the agent's messages on the left with its name and time, each of the thread's cards as an inline card with its Open button (Answer when it waits on the person), the person's own words as a bubble on the right with how far it got under it (**Sent**, **Delivered** or **Read** with its time, and a quiet **Not read** until the agent has opened it), a pull request or new version an agent sent as an inline card, and a reply that asks for a next step with up to four follow-up buttons, the first in ink; a tap is the final answer and moves the card to Answered. A thread opens at its latest message (a long one scrolled to its end, so no message and no choice button sits behind the box), and the box under it is pinned above the dock (or at the pane's foot on a desk). Writing back is a comment on the thread's latest card: it stays in Decide until a final pick.
- `Open the <kind>` (`#inbox/<key>/open`) opens the item, with its own back link to the thread.
- A reply that lands while the board is open shows a notice at the top for 9 seconds; a tap opens its thread. The server sends the same notice to the configured receiver (`notify:`), linking `#inbox/<key>`.
- On a phone the list is one page and a row opens its thread over it. On a desk (1024px and wider) the list sits beside the open thread, and an item opened from its thread sits beside that thread.

## #fleet: the agents talking to each other

The rail's foot carries three entries: **#fleet**, **Agents** (read-only, the Channels room) and **Done**. #fleet is one group channel of every agent's own talk, read from the fleet's own record through `GET /api/channels` — one history, not a second store — and drawn with the thread's own bubbles so it is the same chat with more participants: each message names the agent that said it and when, and its words carry any board link as a **mention chip** that reads the thing's title and opens it (`#tickets/T-29` reads "Move the ticket owner · ticket"), never an id. A mention this board does not hold stays as the agent's own sentence rather than becoming a chip with a made-up title.

#fleet's header carries its **Mute** control, which belongs to this channel and to nothing else; a muted channel keeps every message and says what mute does not do ("Muted · #fleet stays quiet; a message that mentions a report or a ticket still lands in your inbox"). The setting is the board's own, kept in the browser.

The rail above it lists **the people this board hears from** as chips, one per agent with how many threads it holds — a filter of the rail, not another list.

Search looks in #fleet too: a word an agent said shows under **In #fleet** with the words that matched, and a tap opens the channel.

Every message the person wrote says how far it got in one word — Sent, Delivered, Read, and **Replied** once the agent has answered it — with the time, and a quiet Not read until its agent has opened it.

## How to drive and prove it

`node .agents/skills/done-done-verify-sign-off/inbox-shots.mjs <out> <light|dark> <360|390|445|1280|1440>` (journey `inbox`), after `npm run build`. It posts five asks from two agents, a plan, a guided report and a ticket in a throwaway HOME and XDG_*, answers them through the board's API, lets the agents' own waits take them, and has the agents reply with `feedback --reply` (one with a link, one with two `--option` follow-ups) and `--acted`. A headless Chromium of its own over the DevTools protocol at the exact width (844 or 800 high, 2x) captures `inbox`, `thread`, `action`, `answered` (the first follow-up tapped), `notice` (a reply posted while the inbox is open, caught when the 20-second read shows it) and, on a desk, `beside`. Each capture's `.txt` holds the measured sections, chips, pairs and follow-ups; the run fails on a wrong order, a missing pair, sideways overflow, raw ids in the words or a console error.

`done-done run inbox-done` (`inbox-done-proof.sh`, `DONE_ONLY=1 inbox-shots.mjs`) captures the Done gesture with its Undo, the Done view with a search, the thread an agent's reply brought back and the mark still holding after a reload, at 390 and 1280, light and dark, then the Designs capture at 1440 dark and the approved design-pick listing (`designs.1440.dark.png`) in the same run; test/design.test.mjs ("the inbox rail") pins the rail's rows and entries around them.
test/design.test.mjs ("the inbox: one list of every kind") pins the order, the chips and search, the thread's chat (sides, time order, the pull request card) and the Decide rule; "the inbox rail" pins the one-row filters and the short Agents and Done row; `done-done run inbox-chat` (`CHAT_ONLY=1 inbox-shots.mjs` plus the Designs capture, `inbox-chat-proof.sh`) captures the Inbox list with nothing open (`list`, which also holds one row for two cards of one topic), a long thread that scrolls on a phone (`chat`), the grouped thread with its two cards (`group` near its top, `groupend` at its end) and a pull request thread (`pr`) at 390 and 1280, light and dark; test/notifications.test.mjs ("a reply that lands") pins the notice.

A relayed answer: the lead reads it with `sign-off-axi answers` (no wait, no feedback line), which records one read mark, so the thread's bubble says Read, with no reply or card after it. `done-done run answer-receipts` (`RECEIPTS_ONLY=1 inbox-shots.mjs`, 390 and 1280) captures it; test/answer-receipts.test.mjs fails naming the card when the Read step does not advance.
