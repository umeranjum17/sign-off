# Tickets

A ticket is one piece of work (an id, a title, its progress 0 to 100, optionally the ticket it sits under, its priority P0-P3, its project, whether it is on hold and whether the user asked for it), kept beside the board in `<board>.tickets.jsonl` and written by `sign-off-axi ticket`. A card names its ticket with `--ticket <id>` on `raise` and `post` (every `--type` and `--kind`) or `"ticket"` in a message. The link is optional and loose: a card without one, or naming a ticket the board has not got, is exactly as before.

## Sub-features

- `ticket`: with no `--id` it lists every ticket and how many cards name it; `--id` alone shows the ticket and its cards newest first (`kind`, `status`); `--title`, `--progress`, `--parent`, `--priority`, `--project`, `--hold yes|no` and `--asked yes|no` write one. A new ticket needs `--title`; a later write changes only what it gives, and an empty value takes an optional one off. Refused, with nothing written: progress outside 0-100 or not whole, a ticket as its own parent, a blank title, a bad id, a priority outside P0-P3, hold or asked not yes or no, a project name over 40 characters.
- The roll-up: a ticket with tickets under it (an epic's tickets, a ticket's sub-tasks) is as far along as they are on average, worked out on reading (`/api/state`, `ticket --json`); the file keeps what was written.
- `--ticket` on every way a card gets in: `raise`, `post --type decision|design|diagram|interactive|evidence|explainer|signin`, `post --kind <any>` (the flag fills the message's `ticket`; a message naming another ticket is refused), and `post --from` (fills each item with none). `--replace` with `--ticket` is refused: a feed puts `ticket` on its items.
- The Ticket row: on an ask (Decide), a sign-in, an evidence update, a design review, a guided report or plan, a report page and a plan page, one row under what the card says: the ticket's title, its id, a bar and the percent, and a chevron. It opens `#ticket/<id>`. On the update screen its label reads like Proof's (upper case). A card with no ticket, or a ticket not on the board, shows no row.
- Activity: every card's screen (ask, sign-in, update, design review, guided or page report, plan) lists, oldest first, what the board keeps of it: Posted by `<filer>` (a session UUID reads Agent) with its web link, the answer a change replaced, the answer with its note, `<filer>` picked it up (from `wait --filer`), a new round asked for with its note, and comments. A card with only its posting shows none. On an update the label reads upper case, like Proof's. The ticket's page shows its cards' steps together after Linked, each under the card's title, without their postings.
- The Tickets tab (`#tickets`, the dock's and the sidebar's fifth place), as the approved prototype draws it (`signoff-restyle-board/v3`, the Tickets frames; the tree from `signoff-tickets-proto`): the title row with the project filter and the round light-or-dark button; Your asks or System with their counts (only while the board has both); the hero (`Your asks done`, `N of M`, `K this week`, `K moving`) with one square per ticket (done in ink, moving in the middle tone); then In progress, On hold and To do (and Done on a phone), the tickets under no other, P0 first, each row the project's icon or initials, the title (one line on a phone, two on a desk), a P chip where set, and a bar with its percent once it has progress (full when done), else where it stands and when (an epic: how many of its tickets are done); on a phone three rows with See all, and two tiles (Wait on you, Open at P0). A desk is one filled screen: the head across (where you are, the 32px title, one line of the counts, the controls), then three columns: the hero over In progress, On hold over To do, and the open ticket (the first moving one until a row is clicked). Each list scrolls in its card, rests on whole rows and fades its next row while it holds more than it shows; the open ticket fades at its foot.
- A ticket, `#ticket/<id>`: over the list as a sheet on a phone (Close, Escape or a tap above it goes back to where its row was tapped), in the third column on a desk: its project and where it stands, its title in full, Priority, Progress (a bar while partly done, else All done or Not started), Part of (the ticket it sits under, a link) and Last update; then Tickets in this epic or Sub-tasks with how many are done, Linked (every card naming it, not dismissed, newest first; each row says what it is, where it stands and when, and opens where the card lives) and Activity (its cards' steps, newest first, each naming its card). Both widths, both themes, no sideways scroll.

## See all on a phone (0.6.2)

A tap that began on a section's See all or Show fewer does that button's work even when the list moves under the finger before the tap lands (a redraw or the page settling used to hand the tap to the row that slid under it and open a ticket); a tap that began on a row still opens it. Drive `SEEALL_WIDTH=360|390|445|1280|1440 SEEALL_THEME=light|dark done-done run tickets-seeall` (or `node .agents/skills/done-done-verify-sign-off/tickets-seeall-shots.mjs <folder> <theme> <width>`) after building: it expands each section in turn, taps See all on every other one with the list moved 60px under the finger, and saves `sections` (On hold open) and `expanded` (To do open, no ticket sheet); a desk saves `desk` (no See all). Regression: test/design.test.mjs "Tickets on a phone".
