# Tickets

A ticket is one piece of work (an id, a title, its progress 0 to 100, optionally the ticket it sits under, its priority P0-P3, its project, whether it is on hold and whether the user asked for it), kept beside the board in `<board>.tickets.jsonl` and written by `sign-off-axi ticket`. A card names its ticket with `--ticket <id>` on `raise` and `post` (every `--type` and `--kind`) or `"ticket"` in a message. The link is optional and loose: a card without one, or naming a ticket the board has not got, is exactly as before.

## Sub-features

- `ticket`: with no `--id` it lists every ticket and how many cards name it; `--id` alone shows the ticket and its cards newest first (`kind`, `status`); `--title`, `--progress`, `--parent`, `--priority`, `--project`, `--hold yes|no` and `--asked yes|no` write one. A new ticket needs `--title`; a later write changes only what it gives, and an empty value takes an optional one off. Refused, with nothing written: progress outside 0-100 or not whole, a ticket as its own parent, a blank title, a bad id, a priority outside P0-P3, hold or asked not yes or no, a project name over 40 characters.
- The roll-up: a ticket with tickets under it (an epic's tickets, a ticket's sub-tasks) is as far along as they are on average, worked out on reading (`/api/state`, `ticket --json`); the file keeps what was written.
- `--ticket` on every way a card gets in: `raise`, `post --type decision|design|diagram|interactive|evidence|explainer|signin`, `post --kind <any>` (the flag fills the message's `ticket`; a message naming another ticket is refused), and `post --from` (fills each item with none). `--replace` with `--ticket` is refused: a feed puts `ticket` on its items.
- The Ticket row: on an ask (Decide), a sign-in, an evidence update, a design review, a guided report or plan, a report page and a plan page, one row under what the card says: the ticket's title, its id, a bar and the percent, and a chevron. It opens `#ticket/<id>`. On the update screen its label reads like Proof's (upper case). A card with no ticket, or a ticket not on the board, shows no row.
- Card Activity: every card's screen (ask, sign-in, update, design review, guided or page report, plan) lists, oldest first, what the board keeps of it: Posted by `<filer>` (a session UUID reads Agent) with its web link, the answer a change replaced, the answer with its note, `<filer>` picked it up (from `wait --filer`), a new round asked for with its note, and comments. A card with only its posting shows none. On an update the label reads upper case, like Proof's. Ticket Activity details are in [the ticket file format](../../../../docs/file-formats.md#tickets).
- The Tickets tab (`#tickets`, the dock's and the sidebar's fifth place), as the approved prototype draws it (`signoff-restyle-board/v3`, the Tickets frames; the tree from `signoff-tickets-proto`): the title row with the project filter and the round light-or-dark button; two tabs, All tickets and Activity (`#activity`), and a filter button whose sheet has Who asked (Everyone, From you or Fleet upkeep, only while the board has both) beside the projects; the hero (`Tickets done`, `N of M`, `K this week`, `K moving`) with one square per ticket (done in ink, moving in the middle tone); then In progress, On hold and To do (and Done on a phone), the tickets under no other, P0 first, each row the project's icon or initials, the title (one line on a phone, two on a desk), a P chip where set, and a bar with its percent once it has progress (full when done), else where it stands and when (an epic: how many of its tickets are done); on a phone three rows with See all, and two tiles (Wait on you, Open at P0). A desk is one filled screen: the head across (where you are, the 32px title, one line of the counts, the controls), then three columns: the hero over In progress, On hold over To do, and the open ticket (the first moving one until a row is clicked). Each list scrolls in its card, rests on whole rows and fades its next row while it holds more than it shows; the open ticket fades at its foot.
- Board Activity is the Activity tab (`#activity`, the second of the two tabs on Tickets): a timeline grouped by day (Today, Yesterday, then the weekday and date) with a rail down each group, every entry a row built like an In progress card (the project's tile, the title, the P0-P3 chip, the stage chip, the project's name and what moved, the time), with filters for project, priority and type, a count ("7 of 19 events"; changes of one ticket within 2 minutes are one row with the newest change's headline and a +N after what moved), and one empty state when nothing happened yet and another, with Clear filters, when the filters match nothing. On a phone the All tickets screen keeps its Activity card, collapsed above the ticket lists, which opens to its newest rows and ends in See all, to the tab; a desk has the tab only. A row is a button: a tap, or Enter or Space, opens an expanded view (a sheet on a phone, a dialog on a desk) with the full title, the change from and to in plain words, priority, stage, state, Made by (only when the ticket line named a writer with `ticket --filer`; an older line shows none), the ticket as a link that opens it, each pull request as a full https link and the exact time to the second; Escape or Close returns the focus to the row. `/api/state?view=tickets` sends only the newest 200 derived events; the card shows at most 50 of them. Older events remain in ticket records but are not sent in the board-wide feed. Drive the `Tickets read caps...` and `Activity events carry...` API journeys and the `a card names its ticket` browser journey in `test/tickets-api.test.mjs` and `test/design.test.mjs`, and the frames below.
- A ticket, `#ticket/<id>`: over the list as a sheet on a phone (Close, Escape or a tap above it goes back to where its row was tapped), in the third column on a desk: its project and where it stands, its title in full, Priority, Progress (a bar while partly done, else All done or Not started), Part of (the ticket it sits under, a link) and Last update; then Tickets in this epic or Sub-tasks with how many are done, Linked (every card naming it, not dismissed, newest first; each row says what it is, where it stands and when, and opens where the card lives) and Activity. See [the ticket file format](../../../../docs/file-formats.md#tickets) for Activity details. Both widths, both themes, no sideways scroll.

## Empty, loading and error states

Every list, feed and card that can hold nothing shows one shared state (`emptyState` in `web/app-more.js`, `.es` in `web/app.css`): a quiet mark, what goes here, one next step where there is one; a read still on its way shows a skeleton and a line, and a failed read says what failed and offers Try again. The Activity card on a phone is never a bare title: closed it shows its count and newest event. Drive `node .agents/skills/done-done-verify-sign-off/empty-states-shots.mjs <new folder> light|dark 390|1280` (bare and full throwaway boards, reads held and failed through the DevTools Fetch domain; every frame measured in the browser; `TICKETS_SIDE=before SIGN_OFF_REPO=<base build>` captures the base build's Tickets). The CLI refuses a second open ticket for the same project and title, and a `--release` that is a time word; an old twin or time word already on a board is collapsed or dropped when read (`test/tickets-api.test.mjs`).
