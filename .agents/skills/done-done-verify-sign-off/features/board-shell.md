# Board shell

The board page (web/index.html) in the approved new look's foundation: a warm canvas with cards a step lighter and near-black ink, Inter (`web/inter.woff2`, its licence in `web/inter-LICENSE.txt`), a 4px grid with one 16px gutter, a 24px medium title in a 44px row, its own light and dark palettes. Every raw colour, type size, spacing step, corner and shadow lives in `web/tokens.css` (`--tk-*`); the shared parts every screen builds on (the dock and sidebar, the title row and segmented control `.ui-top`/`.ui-seg`, a section head `.ui-sec`, the hero and list cards `.ui-card`/`.ui-hero`/`.ui-list`, a row `.ui-row` with its icon tile `.ui-icon` and P0-P3 chip `.ui-pc`, two stat tiles `.ui-tiles`/`.ui-stat`, the property panel `.ui-props`/`.ui-pr` for Its ask and Ticket) live in `web/ui.css`, with the builders `uiRow` and `uiIcon` in index.html; all three are served beside the page. Screens not yet rebuilt keep their own rules on the new palette (their buttons stay blue until their own slice). `newlook-shots.mjs <folder> [prototype index.html]` (journey `newlook`) captures Today and Reports at 390 and 1280, light and dark, measures them in the browser and, given the approved prototype's page, saves each beside the prototype's own frame (`vs-proto/<state>.<theme>.<width>w.pair.png`). `dock-icons-shots.mjs <folder> <prototype index.html>` (journey `dock-icons`, DOCK_PROTO) measures the dock's and the sidebar's icons on the board and on the prototype alike (each icon's box, its drawing's ink box, filled with no stroke, the colour on screen and idle, centred in its tab or on its label's line, one icon edge and one label edge), opens each tab in turn, and saves the board's dock (390) and sidebar (1280) under the prototype's, light and dark (`vs-proto/<dock|sidebar>.<theme>.<width>w.pair.png`). `tokens-shots.mjs <folder> <light|dark> <390|1280>` (SIGN_OFF_REPO and TOKENS_SIDE=before for a base build) captures Today, Decide, Evidence and a report with the resolved token values and spacing as text; spacing must match between light and dark.

## Sub-features

- Four tabs: Today (`#`), Decide (`#waiting`, the Waiting on you list), Evidence (`#evidence`), Reports (`#reports`). Designs (`#designs`) and Answered (`#answered`) are not tabs: a design ask in Decide opens its review (‹ Today goes back), and Today's foot holds Answered (N) and Designs (N) links; on a phone Designs lights Today. The desk's sidebar lists Designs (N) after Reports, lit on Designs.
- Desk (64rem, 1024px, and wider; narrower is the phone layout): a 240px sidebar on the canvas with the board's blue check mark (20px, the size of an area's icon), Sign-off and the name under it, then a Search field (`#find`) and the four tabs (44px rows, the one on screen raised, each with the dock's filled icon at 20px, in ink on the one on screen); the mark, Search's glass and every area's icon share one left edge, the name, Search and every label the next, and the mark is centred on the name's line (measured by newlook-shots.mjs), Decide's count a red badge and new Reports' a blue one (neither shown at zero), Evidence's a plain number; the tab on show is marked (Decide also for a design or an ask, Today for Answered). Its foot reads "G then a letter jumps" and holds Keyboard shortcuts (?).
- Desk shell (`split()` in web/index.html, used by Decide, Evidence and Reports; Today keeps its own desk dashboard): an area's list (with its header) beside a detail pane, each column scrolling on its own and the page itself not scrolling; the selected row tinted with an accent bar, the focus kept on it, and the detail sliding in from the side the selection moved; a strip under the list names that list's keys. Keys (any width, hinted only on a desk): G then T, D, E or R goes to an area; J and K (or the arrow keys) move through the list; ? opens one centred sheet listing every key; Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes. Prove: `node --test --test-name-pattern='Decide on a desk|Reports on a desk' test/design.test.mjs`, `decide-keys.<theme>.1280w.png` from decide-shots.sh and `reports-desk*.<theme>.1280w.png` from reports-shots.mjs.
- Phone: the floating dock, as the approved new-look prototype draws it: a 56px pill of four round 48px tabs (Today, Decide, Evidence, Reports; icons only, their names for screen readers), 24px over the bottom edge, and the round 56px Search button 12px beside it, the two centred together; the area on screen sits raised in a lighter disc that slides between tabs on a spring, its icon in ink; the icons are the approved prototype's filled drawings (Today a house, Decide a speech bubble with two lines, Evidence a shield with a check, Reports a page with two lines; `ICONS` in web/index.html also holds Tickets' for its tab to come), 24px, the ones not on screen quiet grey; Decide carries a red count of what waits on you and Reports a blue count of what is new, and a count that changes bumps. Search (the button, or `#find`) opens Today with its search box focused. Pages keep 140px under their last row, so it scrolls clear of the bar. Tab pages have no back link; Designs and Answered keep ‹ Today.
- Designs groups contract design reviews by project, with preview, date, status and any saved pick or note. Open a design to approve it or request a change with a note; on multi-screen designs, a native selector targets the note and full-preview link, while designs with no screens accept a design-level note. The preview is readable and scrollable, with a link to open its full asset. At 1280 the board sidebar remains visible with Designs selected; on Today and on Waiting its ask opens the same review.
- After an answer, a toast with Undo sits above the tab bar.
- Today: see [Today](today.md): the title with the light-or-dark button, Up next with Answer in ink, Waiting on you, Cleared today and Proofs today; the line under the tiles opens More, which holds Answered (N), Designs (N), the Leash and Ask the agent; Search is the dock's round button (the sidebar's on a desk).
- Answered: a count and "Each one has gone back to its agent" under the title, a grey TODAY label with Clear all done, then Earlier; each one grouped inset list (also on a desk), newest first; each row shows the question, the answer and when and opens to its full card. A sent-back plan has a green plan square, a green "Sent back" line and "N changes, M decisions · time".
- Decide's list (`#waiting`) is described in [Decide](decide.md): the title Decide with the light-or-dark button, Newest / By priority, the Waiting on you hero, then the asks as rows in one card, Later and Answered.
- An ask (`#decide/<key>`): on a phone a sheet over Decide's list, on a desk beside it; see [Decide](decide.md).
- Reports (`#reports`): see [Reports](reports.md): the date over the large title, "N new · M in all" and More, then Today and Earlier with counts, one card per group of the new look's 68px rows, each a 40px white icon tile, its title with a blue dot while not read, then its project and time (what it is when it names neither); swipe right for read or unread, left to archive with Undo; the tab bar's Reports carries a blue count of what is new. A row with a page opens `#reports/<key>`: ‹ Reports, its form, who asked and time, the title in large type, what it says and decided, then the page in a tile; on a phone it opens over the board, on a desk it reads beside the list (see [Reports](reports.md), Desk).
- Icon: the page links `icon.svg` (favicon), `apple-touch-icon.png` (home screen) and `manifest.webmanifest` (installed name and icons), all served from the board's root; the icon is the family's sign-off tile, a white check on blue.
- Focus rings show on every link and button in both themes.
- Type follows the Spacious design: row titles 17 px regular with 15 px lines under them, never cut off with "…"; section links, back links and Clear all in regular weight; Answered heads in small grey caps with a solid grey check badge per row; Decide named by a small centred title over the question, its stay-quiet line led by a clock and a bold "If you stay quiet:", and Answer on one line in the bar pinned above the tabs on a phone.
- Leash (`name:` and `leash:` lines of the config.yaml beside the board): the name shows under Sign-off in the sidebar brand. Today's More has the Leash: "How much may agents do without asking?", three segments (Ask first, When it matters, Act, then tell) with the chosen one raised, and what it means below. A tap saves `leash: ask|matters|act` into config.yaml (no line: matters); a read-only board shows it without working buttons; a board with no config.yaml shows neither.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open the URL seed-board.sh prints, in a browser; tap an area in the sidebar or the tab bar.

## Driving it

1. Launch as SKILL.md says and keep BASE.
2. For the R2 outcome, capture Waiting and Decide separately at both widths/themes so each shot has one screen claim. Waiting uses `shots.sh BASE "$DONE_DONE_EVIDENCE_ROOT" waiting`; for Decide, open `BASE#decide/look` (the seeded design pick with previews), pick an answer, and save `decide.<theme>.<width>w.png`. Use filenames `<screen>.<theme>.<width>w.png`. Force light with `--color-scheme light` for every light capture; do not rely on the host preference.
3. Designs: open `#designs`, check one project's preview/date/status, then open that design and exercise approve and change with a screen note. Capture `shots.sh BASE "$DONE_DONE_EVIDENCE_ROOT" designs design-review` at 390 and 1280 in light and dark; inspect every capture.
   Today and Answered: `SHOTS_WIDTHS="390 1280" shots.sh BASE "$DONE_DONE_EVIDENCE_ROOT" summary answered` (the `summary` screen is Today); check the Answer, Review and Sign in tiles add up to the Decide count and badge and the Answered rows read newest first.
4. Toast: at 390, open BASE#evidence, click Pass from the page, then screenshot toast.<theme>.390w.png; the toast's bottom is above the tab bar's top.
5. Reports: seed-board.sh posts one report; post one more filed now in the same group and one with no group (`post <board> --kind report --from <json>` with `view` and `preview` from the seeded `page/` folder) so the list shows two sections and a dot.
   Capture `#reports` and `#reports/<key>` at 390 and 1280 in light and dark as `reports.<theme>.<width>w.png` and `report-page.<theme>.<width>w.png`; check the page's ‹ Reports goes back to the row.
   Document scroll regression: `npm run build && node --test --test-name-pattern='report and plan documents use one page scroll' test/design.test.mjs`. Real sandboxed report and plan pages exceed 10,000px, grow after load, and must fit their content with no nested vertical or horizontal scroller at 390 and 1280 (including mobile emulation at 390). The fixture forces a root scrollbar; the measured scrollbar gutter must still be zero, so merely fitting the height cannot pass with a painted inner scrollbar. Report frames fill the reader minus its normal gutters; plan frames fill the main column without an extra inset (the existing answer rail is unchanged).
   Capture proof with `node .agents/skills/done-done-verify-sign-off/document-shots.mjs <output>`: a fresh synthetic release report titled “Latest stable build: muxr 0.2.8” and a plan, both themes and widths, with measured frame/content geometry alongside each capture. On the base source use the same script with `DOCUMENT_BEFORE=1`; it records rather than asserts the broken layout. Inspect every capture. For authorized real-phone proof, use the same synthetic board under the keeper lock, before/after in the same browser viewport, crop system chrome before saving and inspect both images. Never capture an existing personal board. The script stops its own board/browser and removes its scratch state.
   Leash: on `#`, open More from the line under the tiles and click "Act, then tell", check `grep leash: <folder>/config.yaml` prints `leash: act` and Act, then tell stays raised after a reload; capture `leash.<theme>.<width>w.png` at 390 and 1280 (Today's More open) in light and dark.
6. Focus: press Tab until a sidebar link (1280) or a tab (390) has the focus, then screenshot focus.<theme>.<width>w.png (one theme and width in a verify run).
   One check judges at most 10 captures, so keep the Waiting, Decide and Designs captures in separate runs.
   When the outcome is about one width only ("on a computer" or "on a phone"), drive that width alone: SHOTS_WIDTHS=1280 (computer)
   or SHOTS_WIDTHS=390 (phone) for shots.sh, and the toast and focus captures at that width. A capture judged alone must show the whole claim.
7. Icon: `curl -s -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code} %{content_type}\n' BASE{icon.svg,apple-touch-icon.png,manifest.webmanifest}` prints 200 with image/svg+xml, image/png and application/manifest+json; `chrome-devtools-axi eval "() => document.querySelector('link[rel=icon]').href"` ends in /icon.svg.
8. Look at every capture: no text under the tab bar, nothing cut, the right area marked.
9. Foundation (type, controls, tab bar): at 390 and 1280, light and dark, eval every visible button, link, summary and select and list any under 44px in either side (must be none); check dark and light give the same padding, margin and gap on `main`, `.top`, `button`, `.row` and `.tabs a`; at 390 scroll Decide until page text sits behind the dock and check the dock is the 56px pill of 48px tabs beside the 56px Search.
   Journey: `FOUNDATION_THEME=dark FOUNDATION_WIDTH=1280 done-done run foundation` writes decide.after.<theme>.<width>w.png/.txt (tab bar, controls under 44px), today.after.* (type sizes) and spacing.*.json per cell; `FOUNDATION_SIDE=before SIGN_OFF_REPO=<built base checkout>` records the base the same way.
9. Stop the browser session you started; leave the board server, which stops itself.

## Gotchas

- `chrome-devtools-axi wait <ms>` fails; wait with eval "() => new Promise((ok) => setTimeout(ok, 600))".
- An emulated tap can miss a button; click it from the page with eval instead.
- The contract fixture's shot.png is 1x1, so the seed uses the README's phone pictures for media and mocks.
- Dismiss and Clear all: Designs, Evidence and Answered have Select and Clear all in one row under their title (Select: tap cards, then "Dismiss N"), and their cards a × or Dismiss. Reports keeps Mark all as read, Select and Archive all in its More menu, and Dismiss on the report's page. Decide keeps Select in its More menu and Dismiss in an ask's More; its rows have no ×. Prove: on `#answered` click a card's ×, the card goes and the toast says it was dismissed; click `#undo`, it returns.
