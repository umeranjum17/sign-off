<h1>Bots share one computer, and can get their own</h1>
<p>Your answer, as a plan. Every bot works on the one computer that is already set up. You can give any single bot its own computer. When the shared one is busy, a bot can take its own.</p>

<h2>The slices, in order</h2>
<ol>
<li>Show which computer each bot uses. Size: S, half a day. Done when the bot list names the computer for all 12 test bots.</li>

<h2>The one risk worth knowing</h2>
<ul><li>If the shared computer runs out of memory, everything on it stops, every bot at once. The memory run stopped 12 of 12 bots at 16 GB on the desktop class, not on the real engine. Mitigation: a bot takes its own computer once the shared one passes 80% of its memory, and the rollback is one setting that puts every bot back on the shared computer.</li></ul>
