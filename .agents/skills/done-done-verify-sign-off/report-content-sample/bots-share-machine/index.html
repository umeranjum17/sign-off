Bots share one computer, and can get their own

Your answer, as a plan. Every bot works on the one computer that is already set up. You can give any single bot its own computer. When the shared one is busy, a bot can take its own.

The one risk worth knowing

If the shared computer runs out of memory, everything on it stops, every bot at once. That was measured on the desktop class, not on the real engine.
