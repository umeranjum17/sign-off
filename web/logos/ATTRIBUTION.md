# Tool and provider marks

Marks for Claude Code, Anthropic, Codex, OpenCode, Pi, OpenAI, DeepSeek, MiniMax and Ollama are
from [LobeHub Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons) (MIT), recolored to one tone by the
board (drawn as a mask in the text colour).
Product names and marks remain the property of their owners. `generic.svg` is a plain placeholder
for a tool the board does not know, not a brand mark.
