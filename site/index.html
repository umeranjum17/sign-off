One calm page for every question your agents have.

Agents ask; you answer with one tap on your own machine; the answer is saved for the asker to read. Proof of finished work lands on the same page, so you check it instead of taking anyone's word.

Install in 3 steps

From npm, with Node 22.5 or newer. Nothing leaves your machine.

Install

npm install -g sign-off-axi

Set up your project

npx -y sign-off-axi init

Writes .sign-off/ and installs the skill for each coding agent on your PATH.

Open the board

sign-off-axi open

Four places, one tap each

A tab bar on your phone, a sidebar on your desk.

Evidence is not a decision

Decide holds only real asks: things an agent cannot do without your answer. Evidence holds proof, a capture of the running product with the result of the check that made it: Passed, Not run, Failed, Did not finish or Could not check.

A proof update is never counted as something that needs you. Today counts the two separately.

Plans and reports, on their own pages

A plan is what an agent wants to do before it starts: approve or change each section, answer each decision, and send it all back at once.

A report is an explanation you asked for: the answer first, then a diagram and steps to tap through. The board, not the agent's page, owns the answer buttons.

Sign-in cards

When an agent needs you to log in, a sign-in card goes to the top of Decide with the service, what it unlocks, who is waiting and the time left on the link. Tap Open; the agent closes the card when it worked.

It keeps only what matters

A board that fills with noise stops being read, so the rules sit at the door.

  • A question must say why it is asked, what each answer does, which one the agent recommends and what happens if you stay quiet, or it is refused.
  • One decision is one card: the same question again changes nothing.
  • Proof posted as a Pass/Fail question, and proof that is mostly text or a placeholder, is refused: proof goes to Evidence.
  • Dismiss hides a card; Undo brings it back.

Works alone, or with its siblings

The CLI and the skill need no other tool and no supervisor. They connect through one small written contract.

done-done

Verification. Posts its proof as Evidence and holds a run for your Approve or Fix first.

learn-from-mistakes

Retros. Posts each proposed change as a finding for you to Apply or Skip.

first-draft

Design. Its review links show on a design card as real links to the mock-up.

Inside an agent supervisor or a lone agent in a terminal: both post the same asks, plans and reports and read the same answers.
